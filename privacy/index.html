Privacy

Registered business name: Imani Kikuyu Specialty Coffee & Roastery, registration no. BN-79S622VZ.

This notice explains what information Imani Coffee Co. receives when you place an order or sign up for updates.

Information we collect

We receive the details you choose to provide, which may include your name, phone number, email address, delivery area, and order details. Lot-alert and Academy sign-up forms collect the email address you submit.

How we use it

We use order details to confirm, prepare, deliver, and support your order. We use sign-up details to send the updates you requested. You can ask us to stop sending optional updates at any time.

Where information goes

Orders and customer conversations may be handled through WhatsApp. Email sign-up forms are processed by Formspree, our form provider, and delivered to Imani Coffee Co. These providers handle information under their own privacy terms.

How long we keep it

We keep order information only as long as needed to fulfill and support the order and meet applicable record-keeping obligations. We keep sign-up information until you unsubscribe or ask us to remove it, unless we need to retain it for a legal reason.

Contact

For a privacy question or a request about your information, email getimanicoffee@gmail.com.

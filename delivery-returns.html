Delivery & Returns

Delivery

  • Orders are roasted to order and dispatched within 48 hours.
  • After dispatch, delivery takes 24–48 hours in Nairobi and Kiambu, or up to 4 days countrywide to your nearest Pickup Mtaani agent.
  • Delivery is door-to-door in Nairobi and Kiambu, and countrywide to Pickup Mtaani agents.
  • Delivery costs from KSh 120 to an agent point, KSh 250–350 door-to-door in Nairobi, and is free on orders over KSh 2,500.
